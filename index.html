What we do

We take one or two large clients at a time and give each of them the whole team.

Television & film

Unscripted series, specials and feature documentaries, from development through delivery.

Social

Channels and brands built from nothing. For one current client that meant going from zero to 1.5 million followers.

Creative consulting

For marketing and media teams whose content is not landing. We find out why, show you the numbers, and fix it with your people.

Speaking & training

Jay speaks and runs training days for in-house teams. Keynote, half day or full day, on site or remote.

Who makes it

Two founders, with a bench of network crew behind them.

Jay Lyons

Founder · Producer · Director

Jay started out writing and producing theme songs for network television, then moved behind the camera, shooting, directing and producing unscripted shows for MTV, TLC, CBS and Discovery.

In 2019 a passion project became The Long Goodbye, the feature documentary he directed. He has worked on all three screens since: television, film and social.

Sofia J. Lyons

Co-owner · Music

Sofia runs 7 Lyons Media with Jay. She is also a songwriter, and her music is on the soundtrack of The Long Goodbye.

The company stays small on purpose, taking one or two large clients at a time.

Camera

Operators shooting for HBO, TLC, Netflix, Prime Video, Nat Geo and BET.

Producers

Celebrity, first-time and difficult talent. Dancing With The Stars, 90 Day Fiancé, VICE.

Editors

Netflix documentaries, Cake Boss, X-Factor, American Ninja Warrior and viral short-form.

Post

Graphics, color and sound, with credits from the Super Bowl to national commercials.
