What people ask us

How much does it cost?

It depends on what needs making and for how long. We take one or two large clients at a time, so every project is scoped and priced on its own. Tell us what you have in mind and we will tell you what it would take.

How long until our videos go viral?

Nobody can honestly promise viral. What we can do is find out why your content isn’t working and make things people want to watch and share. The work and the numbers on this page show what that looks like when it lands.

We were told to get better at social. Where do we start?

Most teams we meet are in exactly that spot. Start with the three questions above: they give you a straight answer on this page, with no call needed.

Do you offer free consultations?

No. We keep our time for the clients we have. If you are not ready for a full project, the questions above and the list below are free.

Who makes it

Two founders, with a bench of network crew behind them.

Jay Lyons

Founder · Producer · Director

Jay started out writing and producing theme songs for network television, then moved behind the camera, shooting, directing and producing unscripted shows for MTV, TLC, CBS and Discovery.

In 2019 a passion project became The Long Goodbye, the feature documentary he directed. He has worked on all three screens since: television, film and social.

Sofia J. Lyons

Co-owner · Music

Sofia runs 7 Lyons Media with Jay. She is also a songwriter, and her music is on the soundtrack of The Long Goodbye.

The company stays small on purpose, taking one or two large clients at a time.
