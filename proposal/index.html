Audit of 7lyonsmedia.com · 8 & 16 September, re-checked 28 September

What we found on the current site

Everything below was checked on the live site. None of it is anyone’s fault: most of it is the kind of thing that quietly builds up on a WordPress site that was set up years ago and then left to run.

Fix first

Hidden spam links on your pages

At some point, probably around March 2021, someone added spam to the old site: over a hundred hidden links to pharmacy, casino, essay-writing and escort sites, on 7 of your 13 pages. Most are invisible to visitors, but Google sees them, and some text shows up inside your privacy policy. The pages clients normally visit (home, about, reel, contact) are clean. With Microsoft looking, this is the first thing we fix.

Full count 16 Sep: 111 links to 21 spam domains across 7 pages · re-checked 28 Sep: still on the privacy policy, in hidden widget “custom_html-2”
Broken

“Book a consultation” doesn’t open

The main button leads to a private Typeform, so anyone who clicks sees “you can’t access this typeform until its creator says so.”

Still private on 28 Sep
Email

Why your email lands in spam

Your domain is missing two standard email security records that Gmail and Outlook check to trust your mail (called DKIM and DMARC). That fits “lands in spam” exactly. For the error messages, one forwarded bounce will tell us the exact cause.

28 Sep: no DMARC record · no DKIM key found · SPF present
Visibility

No Google Business Profile

When someone searches for a production company in Los Angeles, Google shows a map and a list. You aren’t on it. The top 20 there have 531 reviews between them.

Numbers

Your site undersells you

It says Professor Goes To Prison has 48 or 61 million views. YouTube says 69,013,903. It says Lyon Bros has 40M views; the channel shows about 59.5M. AI search tools quote the old, lower numbers.

Speed

The homepage loads almost 200 MB of video

Including a 98 MB .mov file. On a normal connection that is about 46 seconds. The new site’s whole reel is 12.5 MB, and phones get a 3.9 MB version.

Security

Old logins are visible

Anyone can see the three WordPress usernames (Jay’s and two past builders), and the login page is open. Worth closing even if you keep nothing else.

Search

Small things Google notices

No page descriptions, no main headline on the homepage, test pages (“sample page”, “test”) listed for Google to index, no link-preview images, no analytics, and no links to your own social accounts. The new site fixes all of these.
